# Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse sales performance, revenue generation, and profitability across different business dimensions.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance, transforming sales data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](Sales%20Performance%20Dashboard.jpg)

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Monitor overall revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), and profit.
- Evaluate profitability across different product brands.
- Analyse sales quantities by product colour.
- Compare revenue contributions across regions and product categories.
- Understand profitability across customer income segments.
- Track monthly profit performance and identify trends.
- Provide interactive filtering for more focused analysis.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks five core business metrics:

| KPI | Reported Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $3.11M |
| Cost of Goods Sold | $2.17M |
| Total Profit | $931.91K |
| Total Customers | 250 |
| Profit Margin | 30.0% |

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes six major analytical visualisations:

**1. Profit by Brand**
- Compares profitability across Apple, Lenovo, Samsung, HP, Asus, and Dell.
- Identifies the brands contributing most to business profit.

**2. Quantity Sold by Colour**
- Analyses product quantities sold across White, Gray, and Black.
- Highlights differences in product colour demand.

**3. Revenue by Category**
- Compares revenue contributions from Accessories and Electronics.
- Provides a breakdown of category-level revenue performance.

**4. Revenue by Region**
- Evaluates revenue generated across North, East, South, and West.
- Helps identify stronger and weaker regional markets.

**5. Profit by Income Level**
- Compares profitability across Low, Medium, and High income customer segments.
- Identifies the customer segment contributing the most profit.

**6. Monthly Profit Trend**
- Tracks monthly profit performance across January, February, and March.
- Provides visibility into changes in profitability over time.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers that allow users to filter the analysis by:

- Region
- Product Category

These interactive filters make it easier to explore business performance across different segments.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the displayed dashboard results:

- The business generated approximately **$3.11M in revenue**, with **$931.91K in profit** and a **30% profit margin**.
- Apple recorded the highest profit among the displayed brands.
- White products recorded the highest sales quantity, at approximately 6.1K units.
- The North region generated the highest revenue among the four regions.
- Medium-income customers contributed the highest total profit.
- March recorded the highest monthly profit, following a decline in February.
